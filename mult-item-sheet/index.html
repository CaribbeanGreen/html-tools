Adding icons: save the icons from the RoR2 wiki (or crop in-game screenshots), keeping the item name in the file name, e.g. Crowbar.png. Tap + Add icons, select them all at once, and each lands on its item. Tap a single tile to set or replace just that one.

Keeping your icons in an export: icons live in your browser, so a plain export has none. Open the sheet as a downloaded .html file, add your icons there, then tap Save copy with icons. That copy has every picture built in and works offline on any device.

Images you add are saved in this browser only. MUL-T's Auto-Nailgun hits many times per second, so on-hit items (Ukulele, AtG, Tri-Tip, Leeching Seed) are the core of the build. Use Retool to carry two equipment.
